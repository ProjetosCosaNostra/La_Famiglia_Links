BUYING GUIDE · SKINCARE

What to check before buying a vitamin C serum.

A front-label percentage is not the whole decision. A useful comparison also looks at formula format, packaging, routine fit and what remains unverified.

Research status: category frameworkPersonal testing: none claimedRetailer link: not connected
01

Start with the question you’re actually asking.

If your goal is simply to add a morning antioxidant step, the most useful comparison may be routine compatibility and tolerability. If you are comparing two specific formulas, ingredient format, packaging and usage instructions become more important.

02

Packaging matters because stability matters.

Vitamin C formulas can differ in stability. Opaque or air-restrictive packaging may be relevant depending on the specific formula, but packaging alone does not prove performance.

03

A percentage does not tell the whole story.

Different vitamin C derivatives and formula systems behave differently. A higher number on the label should not automatically be treated as a better product.

04

Routine fit can be more useful than a “best” label.

Texture, irritation risk, manufacturer directions and the other products already in your routine can change which option makes sense.

05

Keep the unknowns visible.

Without product-specific evidence or direct testing, we should not claim wear, feel, irritation rate, shelf life or visible results. Those remain product-by-product questions.

Comparison checklist

  • Vitamin C format
  • Concentration, if disclosed
  • Packaging
  • Manufacturer directions
  • Fragrance / sensitivities
  • Texture and routine placement
  • Price per usable amount
  • What evidence is still missing
