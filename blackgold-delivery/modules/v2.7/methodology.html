HOW WE WORK

Research that shows its boundaries.

BlackGold Beauty Finds is designed around a simple rule: if we do not know something, the interface should not pretend that we do.

01

Define the decision

Every page begins with a shopper question, not with an affiliate link.

02

Separate evidence states

Manufacturer information, retailer information, public feedback and direct testing are not treated as the same thing.

03

Keep limitations visible

Subjective fit, missing evidence and product-specific unknowns stay attached to the recommendation.

04

Verify commercial routes

Retailer and affiliate destinations remain locked until the real URL, country and program status are checked.

05

Measure without manipulating

Campaign attribution should tell us what content helped—not create fake scarcity or pressure.

06

Correct publicly when needed

Research pages should have a clear correction route when source information changes.

AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE

Commercial relationships should be easy to see.

This staging version has no active affiliate links. When affiliate relationships are connected, qualifying purchases may generate a commission without increasing the shopper’s price. That relationship will be disclosed on relevant pages.
