COMMERCIAL TRANSPARENCY

Affiliate relationships do not get to hide behind the research.

BlackGold Beauty Finds separates product research from commercial routing. A retailer being observed or cited does not mean an affiliate link is active.

01

Current staging state

The current staging experience contains no unlocked affiliate routes. Product research may cite retailer pages as sources while shopper exits remain locked until a separate affiliate destination is verified.

02

When affiliate links are activated

If a verified affiliate route is enabled in a future public release, BlackGold Beauty Finds may earn a commission from qualifying activity. The retailer or affiliate network pays that commission; BlackGold Beauty Finds does not add a separate affiliate fee to the shopper.

03

What does not change

Affiliate eligibility does not change the requirement to show the research source, verification date, limitations and commercial status. An affiliate-ready product is not automatically ranked above a non-affiliate product.

04

Retailer Gate

Outbound commercial routes are deny-by-default. The Retailer Gate requires a product-specific destination, HTTPS, a verification date and a verified registry state before a shopper exit can be enabled.

THE SHORT VERSION

Research first. Commercial route second.

If the route has not been verified, the site should show it as locked rather than inventing a destination or sending the shopper to an uncertain page.
