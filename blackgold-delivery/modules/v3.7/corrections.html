CORRECTIONS · RESEARCH MAINTENANCE

A correction should change the record, not just the sentence.

When product research changes, the source, verification date, known facts, unknowns, limitations and commercial state should be reviewed together.

01

What can trigger a correction

A changed retailer page, discontinued item, renamed product, new size or formulation information, broken source, incorrect product match, stale affiliate destination or unsupported claim can all require a correction.

02

Evidence before wording

The first step is to verify the underlying source. Copy should not be changed to sound more confident than the evidence supports.

03

Verification date moves with the evidence

A record should receive a new verification date only when the relevant source was actually checked again. A cosmetic edit should not make old research appear fresh.

04

Commercial routes are independent

A corrected research record does not automatically validate an affiliate destination. Retailer Gate verification remains a separate step.

05

Current staging route

This staging build does not expose a public correction form or public contact endpoint. Corrections are handled through the project’s internal research workflow before public release.

06

Public release requirement

Before a public launch, this page should expose a real public correction/contact route. Until that route exists, the staging build should not claim that shoppers can submit corrections directly.

CORRECTION CONTRACT

Source → record → page → commercial state.

A correction is complete only when the affected layers agree again.
