THE CURRENT ECOSYSTEM

Every section has a job. Nothing needs to pretend it is finished.

The staging ecosystem is intentionally limited to features that exist now. External projects or commercial destinations should only appear here after their public release state is verified.

01 · DISCOVER

Beauty Index

Browse the eight current US beauty products with source dates, limitations and retailer research status.

02 · DECIDE

Buying Guides

Start with a purchase question and use the current product dataset as decision context.

03 · COMPARE

Comparison Desk

Keep up to three research choices side by side instead of flattening them into one universal score.

04 · VERIFY

Research Ledger

See publisher, source type, verification date and evidence freshness in one place.

05 · CAMPAIGN

Campaign Library

Intent-focused campaign pages connect questions to the current product research without bypassing commercial safeguards.

06 · COMMERCIAL CONTROL

Affiliate Workbench

Internal staging tool for checking proposed retailer and affiliate routes before anything is unlocked.
