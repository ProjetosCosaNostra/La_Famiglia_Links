TERMS · CURRENT STAGING BUILD

Use the research as context, not as a guarantee.

BlackGold Beauty Finds is designed to help shoppers compare beauty products and buying decisions. The site does not replace professional medical advice, direct product testing or the retailer’s current product information.

01

Research scope

Product pages can document retailer listings, product formats, labeled details, research dates and limitations. They should not be read as proof that a product will produce a specific personal result.

02

Availability and details can change

Retailers can change availability, product pages, size options, prices, promotions and product details. The visible verification date is part of the research record and should be considered before a purchase decision.

03

No medical diagnosis

Skincare and beauty information is general shopping context, not medical diagnosis or treatment advice. Questions about allergies, reactions, medical conditions or treatment should be handled with an appropriate qualified professional.

04

External destinations

A verified retailer link leaves the BlackGold Beauty Finds experience. Purchases, returns, delivery, warranties, payments and retailer service are governed by the external retailer’s own terms.

05

Affiliate relationships

Affiliate relationships are disclosed separately. Commercial eligibility does not override the research boundary or guarantee product quality, fit or performance.

06

Corrections

Research can become stale or contain mistakes. The Corrections page explains the staging correction path and the evidence fields that should be reviewed when a record changes.

DECISION RULE

Check the current retailer information before checkout.

BlackGold Beauty Finds can organize the decision, but the retailer’s current listing remains important for the exact product, instructions, availability and transaction.
