RESEARCH → DECISION → COMMERCE

Six gates before a recommendation becomes a shopper exit.

  1. 01

    Define the shopper decision.

    Start with the question, never the affiliate link.

  2. 02

    Record the observed source.

    Keep retailer, source type and verification date attached to the record.

  3. 03

    Separate known from inferred.

    Do not turn shade fit, comfort, longevity, irritation or performance into facts without evidence.

  4. 04

    Keep limitations beside the product.

    Unknowns stay visible in the index, product page, comparison and guide.

  5. 05

    Verify commerce separately.

    Observed retailer availability does not unlock an affiliate destination; Retailer Gate stays deny-by-default.

  6. 06

    Correct when evidence changes.

    Update the research record before recommendations or campaigns.

EVIDENCE STATES

Four labels the interface must not blur together.

OBSERVED

Source says it.

A recorded source supports the statement.

CONTEXTUAL

Useful, not universal.

Routine, hair, shade, scent, climate or technique can change fit.

UNKNOWN

Not established.

Unverified outcomes stay unclaimed.

COMMERCIAL

Route state is separate.

A verified source does not unlock a retailer link.
