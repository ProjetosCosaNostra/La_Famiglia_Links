RESEARCH → DECISION → COMMERCE

Six gates.

  1. 01

    Define the decision.

    Start with the shopper question, not an affiliate link.

  2. 02

    Record the source.

    Keep retailer, source type and verification date attached.

  3. 03

    Separate known from inferred.

    Unverified fit, wear, comfort or performance stay unclaimed.

  4. 04

    Keep limitations visible.

    Unknowns stay beside the recommendation.

  5. 05

    Verify commerce separately.

    Retailer Gate stays deny-by-default.

  6. 06

    Correct when evidence changes.

    Update research before campaigns or recommendations.
