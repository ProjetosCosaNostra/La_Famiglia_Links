HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

Evidence first. Commercial intent second.

Every product page should make it possible to distinguish what was observed, when it was checked, what remains unknown, and whether a commercial route is actually verified.

CORE RULEIf the evidence changes, the interface should change with it.

A product can remain researched while its retailer route is locked. A source can become stale without turning into a recommendation. A correction can change one record without hiding the history of the decision.

RESEARCH → DECISION → COMMERCE

Six gates before a recommendation becomes a shopper exit.

  1. 01

    Define the shopper decision.

    Research starts with a concrete question: richer moisturizer or not, gloss or treatment, anti-frizz product or tool. The affiliate link is not the research question.

  2. 02

    Record the observed source.

    Product name, retailer, source type and verification date stay attached to the record. Price is intentionally excluded from the stored research dataset because it can change quickly.

  3. 03

    Separate known from inferred.

    Retailer-listed format, ingredients or sizes can be documented. Personal comfort, shade fit, longevity, irritation and performance are not upgraded into facts without evidence.

  4. 04

    Keep limitations beside the product.

    Unknowns do not live in a distant disclaimer. They remain visible in the Beauty Index, product page, comparison view and buying guide.

  5. 05

    Verify the commercial route separately.

    Observed retailer availability is not the same as an approved affiliate destination. The Retailer Gate stays deny-by-default until the route is marked verified.

  6. 06

    Correct the record when evidence changes.

    Source changes, retailer changes and factual corrections should propagate to the research record before they propagate to recommendations or campaigns.

EVIDENCE STATES

Four labels the interface must not blur together.

OBSERVED

Source says it.

A current retailer or other recorded source supports the specific statement.

CONTEXTUAL

Useful, but not universal.

Routine, hair type, shade, scent, climate or technique can change the decision.

UNKNOWN

Not established.

The build does not pretend to know wear, comfort, results or other product-specific outcomes that were not verified.

COMMERCIAL

Route state is separate.

A verified source does not unlock a retailer link. Commercial activation has its own registry and gate.
